The Kyoto Design Philosophy

We do not build loud products. We build deliberate, minimalist, and human-centric software.

⚡

Ma (間) — Meaningful Silence

Ma is the art of restraint. Steve Jobs called it "editing ruthlessly." GenPark agents do not fill every second with noise. An agent that pauses, reflects, and listens represents true craft. Every line of text, prompt, and UI token is sculpted to serve, never to distract.

"The most profound technologies are the ones that disappear." — Steve Jobs
🌀

Shu-Ha-Ri (守破離) — The Mastery Arc

Every AI skill in GenPark follows this path:

  • 守 (Shu): Follow the form. We provide structured, standardized SKILL.md rules to guarantee immediate capability.
  • 破 (Ha): Break the form. Operators combine, tweak, and overlay capabilities custom-fitted to their workflow.
  • 離 (Ri): Transcend the form. The agent synthesizes completely new skills programmatically based on user intent.
🌸

Ichigo Ichie (一期一会) — This Session, Once

Every meeting between operator and assistant is singular. It will never happen again in exactly this configuration of context, urgency, and background. GenPark agents read the room: timezone, history, tone, and environment, crafting direct personalized interactions rather than canned templates.

"Treat every session as a one-time tea ceremony." — Founder's Reference
