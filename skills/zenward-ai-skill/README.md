## 🌟 Highlights & Core Features

- **Checkout Warranty Upsell Suppressor**: Intercept predatory 80%-margin retailer warranty popups and verify cardholder complimentary extended warranties
- **Living Vault of Ownership**: Bind receipts, serial numbers, and warranty horizons to alert users at T-60 and T-30 days before OEM expiry
- **CPSC & OEM Safety Recall Sentinel**: Continuously monitor global recall registries (CPSC, NHTSA, RAPEX) to trigger free remedy replacements
- **1-Click Claim Dossier Compiler**: Assemble itemized receipts, card statement line items, serial photos, and repair diagnostic sheets to file claims in 60s
