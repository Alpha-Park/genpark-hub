## 🌟 Highlights & Core Features

- **Forfeiture Cliff Sentinel**: Tracks year-end December 31 / March 15 deadlines and IRS carryover ceilings ($640–$660) to prevent employers from confiscating pre-tax balances.
- **Past-Year Multi-Merchant Receipt Mining**: Scans Amazon, Target, and pharmacy receipts against IRS § 213(d) eligible UPCs to recover forgotten cash reimbursements directly to your bank account.
- **Automated Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN)**: Generates clinical justification packages for high-value wellness hardware (red-light therapy, air purifiers, standing desks) for telehealth sign-off.
- **Dynamic Knapsack Basket Optimizer**: Mathematically solves multi-merchant cart allocations to exhaust residual balances on high-utility medical gear with zero waste.

## 🚀 Quick Start & CLI Usage

```bash
# Ingest FSA balance and check forfeiture exposure
npx genpark run zenflex --balance 1250 --admin "HEALTHEQUITY" --deadline "2026-12-31"

# Scan past Amazon and Target orders for retroactive cash reimbursement
npx genpark run zenflex --scan-receipts --lookback 12 --output-claims
```

## 🛠 MCP Tools Exposed

- `zenflex_audit_cliff`: Computes at-risk pre-tax balances against statutory carryover caps.
- `zenflex_scan_receipts`: Cross-references order histories against IRS § 213(d) databases.
- `zenflex_generate_lmn`: Prepares compliant Letters of Medical Necessity for clinician verification.
- `zenflex_knapsack_basket`: Solves optimal product selection for expiring FSA balances.
