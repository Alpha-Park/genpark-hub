## 🌟 Highlights & Core Features

- **Biomarker Telemetry Synthesis**: Ingest Quest/Labcorp panels, genetics, and wearable data to map systemic nutrient deficiencies and optimal longevity ranges
- **Bioavailability Form Auditor**: Evaluate nutrient chemical forms to eliminate low-absorption compounds (e.g. Magnesium Oxide) in favor of high-bioavailability chelates (Glycinate, Threonate)
- **Nutrient Antagonism & Circadian Arbiter**: Eliminate absorption competition (Zinc vs. Copper, Iron vs. Calcium) and partition supplements into morning, afternoon, and evening protocols
- **USP/NSF Purity Gatekeeper**: Filter out heavy metal contaminants, unverified proprietary blends, and low-grade fillers via third-party certification databases
- **Dynamic Titration & Retest Procurement**: Tie bottle fulfillment quantities to 180-day blood draw milestones and auto-titrate dosages based on longitudinal delta vectors

- `zenvital_audit`: Non-destructive state assessment and discrepancy scanner.
